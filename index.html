Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about solar installation, subsidy, net metering, and warranties.

Under the current government scheme for residential consumers, you receive ₹30,000 for a 1 kW system, ₹60,000 for a 2 kW system, and a maximum of ₹78,000 for 3 kW and higher systems. Mayapur Solar handles 100% of the portal registration and bank subsidy credit process for you.

As a rule of thumb, approximately 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free rooftop space is required for every 1 kW of solar panel installation. For example, a 3 kW system needs around 250 to 300 sq. ft.

With a bi-directional Net Meter installed by WBSEDCL, electricity generated by your solar panels powers your home first. Any surplus energy flows into the main power grid and earns you credit units. At night or on cloudy days, you draw from these earned credits, reducing your bill up to 90%.

Yes! Modern Mono PERC and TOPCon panels use diffuse daylight and still generate between 25% and 50% of their peak power on cloudy days. Over the course of the full year, the sunny days balance out your overall generation.

Solar panels have a performance lifespan of 25 to 30 years with a 25-year manufacturer warranty. Inverters typically carry a 5 to 10-year warranty, and Mayapur Solar provides full service warranty on installation and workmanship.

Yes! Government-backed low-interest solar rooftop loans (as low as ~7% p.a.) are available from leading public and private banks through the national portal. We assist you with instant documentation.
